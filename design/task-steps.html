On the board, celebrations burst out from around the whole card. Ticking the ring is the smallest, a card moving forward is bigger and gives the card a bump, and finishing a task is the biggest. In the open card, Mark done bursts from the button.

Round 4: celebrations (pick A, B or C)

  • A · Confetti: a small ring of confetti bits pops out of the button and falls. Classic party popper.
  • B · Sparks: a quick firework of short sparks plus a ring pulse. The streaks droop and fall like a firework.
  • C · Popper + cheer: confetti shoots upward and a tiny word floats up: "Nice!", "One down", "Crushed it". This is the "atta boy" one. The words are picked at random, so it doesn't get stale.
  • Round 5: everything is bigger. On the board the burst comes out from around the whole card outline (with a quick outline flash), not just the ring.
  • Round 6: pieces now fall under gravity with a little sway, and the Duration slider (0.6–3s) sets how long every burst lasts. Longer means they fall further. Pick the duration that feels right.
  • Round 7: the duration now goes up to 5s. Steps view, celebration style and duration all live on a Settings page, under a new Settings item at the bottom of the sidebar. Choosing a style, or letting go of the duration slider, plays a preview on the page. Dark mode stays in the sidebar for quick access.
  • Three sizes: step done < card moves forward (plus a card bump) < task done. It only plays on forward moves; dragging a card backwards or undoing a step is silent.
  • Rules for all: it plays at the button or card you used, never across the screen. It lasts as long as the Duration slider says, never blocks clicks, and is off for anyone whose system asks for reduced motion. No sound for now; a soft pop could be a later setting.
  • Points and the Oregon Trail travellers come later. Steps already record when they were finished, so points can be worked out later from that history.

Decided in round 1 (Nitin)

  • Stepper layout for the expanded card. Steps are cards in a horizontal strip with snap scrolling: trackpad/touch swipe, mouse drag, side arrows, dots, ←/→. Neighbouring steps peek in at the edges, so you can see there's more on each side.
  • Completing a step on a To do task moves it to In progress, from the big card or the collapsed check.
  • Steps are editable, but behind an Edit steps button because it's not an everyday action. The editor shows steps as bubbles: hold and drag to reorder, click text to rename, × to delete, plus add. Nothing changes until Save, and Cancel/Esc throws the edits away.

Decided in round 2 (Nitin)

  • Collapsed check: option 1, the leading circle. The gap above and below the step line is now even (8px each), and the ring is centred on the first line of text.
  • Steps view, Stepper / Timeline: first in the sidebar above Dark mode, moved to Settings in round 7. It's remembered per browser, like the theme. Both views share Mark done, Undo and Edit steps.

Labels (unchanged)

  • The current step is the first one that isn't done. Collapsed In progress cards call it First action, Next action or Last action, depending on where that step sits in the list. Reordering steps in the editor can change the label.
  • A progress bar (2/5) shows on To do and In progress cards once any step is done.

Choices I made, push back if wrong

  • In the editor, finished steps are locked at the top (shown, not draggable). They're history. To reorder one, undo it first.
  • Ticking the collapsed check shows a 5-second Undo toast, because it's a small target next to a card that opens on click.
  • The collapsed check appears on In progress cards only, since those are the only collapsed cards that show the step text.
  • Keyboard: in the editor, focus a bubble and press Alt+↑/↓ to move it, or Enter to rename it.

Implementation sketch (after approval)

  • New table task_steps (id, task_id, position, text, done_at). Each existing first_action becomes step 1. This is a schema change, so rm recenter.db + re-seed.
  • Task payloads include steps. Endpoints: complete/undo a step (complete also moves To do → In progress), and replace the step list (the editor's Save).
  • Bigger than REC-27, so it wants its own story from the lead. REC-10's planner will write these steps.
